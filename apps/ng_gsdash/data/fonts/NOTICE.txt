G's Dash look fonts

The atlases in this folder (*.json) are glyph images baked from these open fonts by
tools/bake_gsdash_fonts.py. Each is used under its own licence, included here:

  gs_m_*   Saira (Omnibus-Type)                     SIL Open Font License 1.1   LICENSE-Saira.txt
  gs_gt_*  Chakra Petch (Cadson Demak)              SIL Open Font License 1.1   LICENSE-ChakraPetch.txt
  gs_h_txt Jost (Owen Earl / indestructible type*)  SIL Open Font License 1.1   LICENSE-Jost.txt
  gs_s_*   Titillium Web (Accademia di Belle Arti   SIL Open Font License 1.1   LICENSE-TitilliumWeb.txt
           di Urbino)
  gs_p_px  G's Dash pixel font (original, drawn for   -                     px.js
           this app in px.js; tools/bake_px_font.js)
  gs_d_*   DSEG7 / DSEG14 Classic (keshikan)        SIL Open Font License 1.1   LICENSE-DSEG.txt
  gs_c_*   Liberation Sans Bold / Regular and       SIL Open Font License 1.1   LICENSE-Liberation.txt
           Liberation Serif Bold (Red Hat; Arimo /
           Tinos digitized data by Google)

No font is a car maker's: the looks are original designs in the spirit of their genres, with no
brand fonts, logos or emblems. iDrive CCC redraws the first iDrive's start screen with these open
fonts and drawing code; no picture or font of the car maker's is used.
