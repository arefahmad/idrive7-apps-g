# Changelog

## G's Dash 0.27.0

- Removed two looks: Aura and Next Gen, with their cluster logos (shader and logo_aura.png /
  logo_nextgen.png), HUD styles and cluster-dial choices. Thirteen looks remain.
- If one of them was the chosen look it becomes M Style when the app starts; as a favourite it simply
  drops out of the list.

## G's Dash 0.26.0

- New look: Pod '04, the dash as a mid-2000s pocket music player, in original art (no maker's name,
  logo, font or icons). A glossy white front with a backlit blue-grey screen and a touch wheel.
- The screen plays your drive as Now Playing: the gear as the track number, the speed as the title, the
  drive mode as the artist, the session as the album, and the revs as the progress bar - rpm elapsed on
  the left, rpm still to go to the shift point on the right - turning amber then red at the shift point
  ("Shift Up!" in the title bar). Play or pause in the title bar as you move or stop; oil and coolant as
  level bars. Beside it a menu list of the g-force peaks, its top row highlighted.
- On the wheel the revs fill round its ring, the gear sits on the centre button, and a fingertip dot
  moves with the g-force.
- Its cluster logo is a charcoal G with blue stripes on the white front over a touch wheel (a shader,
  with logo_pod.png as the fallback). The HUD uses blue bars on Sim Racing's glyphs; the cluster
  dials use Lumen's.

## G's Dash 0.25.0

- New look: Arcade, the dash as an 80s coin-op racing cabinet, in original art (no game's name, logo,
  characters or sprites). A night road in pseudo-3D runs to a blocky skyline under a pixel moon; its
  rumble strips and lane dashes rush past with your speed, it bends against the cornering g, and a pixel
  car drawn for G's Dash drifts with it, tail lights flaring when you brake. CRT scanlines over it all.
- The cabinet's HUD: 1UP with a score that counts up as you drive, HI-SCORE (beat 050000 and it follows
  you, with NEW HI-SCORE!), 2UP with INSERT COIN blinking, CREDIT 00. The speed in big pixel numerals,
  the gear as the STAGE, the revs as a POWER bar of blocks, oil and coolant as block bars, a BONUS card
  of the g-force peaks. Standing still, PLAYER 1 / PRESS START blinks over the road; at the shift point,
  SHIFT UP!.
- Its cluster logo is the badge in big pixels with a drop shadow in a neon frame, with scanlines (a
  shader, with logo_arcade.png as the fallback). The HUD uses chunky pixel blocks on Pixel's glyphs; the
  cluster dials use Pixel's.

## G's Dash 0.24.0

- Theme's gallery scrolls: four looks a row at full size (170 px cards again, not seven narrow ones),
  as many rows as fit, the rest a drag (or a turn of the controller) away, a row at a time so no card is
  ever cut. A bar beside it shows where you are; it opens on the current look's row.
- A look's colours are picked only on Options' Theme card (the one with Change look), no longer on the
  chosen card in the gallery.

## G's Dash 0.23.0

- New look: Heist, the dash as an open-world crime game's HUD, in original art (no game's name, logo,
  font, characters or catchphrases). A posterized sunset city behind it: a banded sun, a skyline of lit
  windows, palm trees.
- A round radar in the corner: streets scroll past your car's arrow as you drive, with blips, and the
  revs as a ring round it (green, yellow over the shift lights, red past the shift point).
- The speed in big outlined type, with a five-star wanted level that climbs with the speed (one star
  from 35 mph, five from 95). From three stars searchlights sweep the sky and the stars flash; from
  four, red and blue lights pulse at the edges of the screen.
- The gear in a box, oil and coolant as bars, a STATS card of the g-force peaks, and mission-style
  subtitles at the bottom ("Shift up!", "Lose the heat.").
- Its cluster logo is a white G with a thick black outline over a sunset with a skyline, a palm and three
  stars (a shader, with logo_heist.png as the fallback). The HUD uses green / yellow / red bars with red
  and blue flashes on Sim Racing's glyphs; the cluster dials use Sim Racing's.
- Theme shows thirteen looks, seven a row.

## G's Dash 0.22.0

- New look: Next Gen, the dash in the style of a current games console's home screen, in original art
  (no maker's name, logo, button symbols or artwork). Soft bands of light flow slowly across a deep blue
  background as glowing particles drift up; frosted-glass cards with round corners; the tach as a thin
  glowing ring with the speed large in its middle; the gear and oil / coolant in their own cards.
- A light bar along the bottom brightens with the revs and turns amber, then red, at the shift point.
- An achievement card slides in when a session peak (launch, hard stop, cornering) is beaten.
- Its cluster logo is a glossy white G over flowing blue light with a light bar (a shader, with
  logo_nextgen.png as the fallback). The HUD uses thin ice-blue segments on Vector's glyphs; the cluster
  dials use Lumen's.
- Theme shows twelve looks, six a row.

## G's Dash 0.21.0

- New look: Pocket, the dash as a late-80s pocket game console in original art (no maker's name, logo
  or artwork). A light grey body, a slate bezel with a red power light round a four-shade green
  dot-matrix screen, a cross pad, two round buttons, select and start. On the screen, as a game would
  draw it: the rpm as a block bar ("SHIFT!" blinking past the shift point), the speed in big pixel
  numerals, the gear in a box, oil and coolant bars, a g-meter target with the peaks and TIME, and a
  little car driving along a road under scrolling hills, faster with the car's speed.
- Its cluster logo is the badge in green dots on the console's screen (a shader, with logo_pocket.png
  as the fallback). The HUD uses dot-matrix green blocks on the Pixel look's glyphs; the cluster dials
  use Pixel's Handheld colours.
- Theme shows eleven looks as six a row.

## G's Dash 0.20.0

- New look: Desktop '98, the dash as a late-90s desktop in original art (no maker's name, logo or
  artwork). Grey bevelled windows with navy gradient title bars on a teal desktop: the speed in a
  sunken field in big pixel numerals, the rpm as a block progress bar ("Revving..."), and past the
  shift point a "Shift now!" warning dialog; the gear in its own window with the drive mode as radio
  buttons; oil and coolant as progress bars; a black performance-monitor g-meter with a green grid and
  a list of peaks; a taskbar with a G button, the windows and the time in its tray.
- Its cluster logo is the badge in a bevelled window with an arrow pointer (a shader, with
  logo_desktop.png as the fallback). The HUD uses progress-bar blocks on the Pixel look's glyphs; the
  cluster dials use Stock's.

## G's Dash 0.19.0

- New look: Aura, anime battle energy in original art. The tach ring sits in an aura of flames that
  powers up with the revs: calm blue-white at idle, blazing gold as the rpm climbs, lightning
  crackling round it from the shift lights on. The flames lean with the lateral g and shed rising
  embers; speed lines streak past, more of them the faster the car goes.
- Aura's readouts: the rpm as a power level, the gear in a glowing energy orb, an amber scanner
  g-meter with a sweeping beam, battle time. Its cluster logo is a gold G in rising flames with
  lightning (a shader, with logo_aura.png as the fallback). The HUD uses Aura colours on M Style's
  glyphs; the cluster dials use GT's.
- Theme shows nine looks as five a row; long names fit the narrower cards.

## G's Dash 0.18.2

- Logo can follow the driving mode. Off by default, so one Logo behaves as before. On, Comfort, Sport,
  Sport+ and any other mode the car reports (Eco Pro, Adaptive and the rest of the same badge) each have
  a GIF slot. An empty slot uses the main Logo.
- The mode is the Drive badge: `FES.statusFESVehicleDynamicSwitchHMI.actualValueModeDrivingExperience`.
- Drive's right panel can be that Logo, beside the G-meter and Spotify. The G-meter stays the default.
- Stage 1 / 2 / 3 is original art for Comfort, Sport and Sport+. Use your own GIFs for any other picture.
